How to use the CDP

A customer data platform does three jobs: it knows every customer, decides who to talk to next, and does the sending. Every screen in this app is one of those jobs. The goal is one number: $100k in sales a month, with profit coming from repeat orders.

Your daily routine

About 10 minutes
  1. Open Home. The checklist at the top shows where you are today.
  2. Data refreshes itself when you or Sy open the app: new Shopify orders on open and every 30 minutes, Omnisend consent once a day. Only yesterday's Meta spend needs logging (until Meta is connected).
  3. Work the moves from #1 down. Press Prepare for Sy's approval: the app syncs the list, builds the segment, creates the draft from that audience's template and sends a test to you. Sy then approves and sends. "What's due when" on Home (and your Google Calendar) shows the date for each step.
  4. Sy approves anything waiting (shown under Needs attention), then sends.
  5. Done. Tomorrow the list re-ranks itself from the new data.

Your weekly check

Mondays, about 20 minutes
  1. Growth levers on Home: which of the 4 numbers is off track (new customers, CAC, first-order value, repeat orders)?
  2. Insights → Revenue & profit: last 30 days. Is net margin heading to 15%?
  3. Insights → Path to $100k: this month's targets for new customers and repeat orders.
  4. Team tasks on Home: reassign or close anything stuck.
  5. Meta budget: scale only if 7-day CAC is at or under $70; Home tells you when to cut.

Email templates: one per audience

Picked automatically. You never choose a template.

Each audience has a fixed template name in Omnisend: CF · <audience> · v1. The app picks the newest template whose name starts that way, and marks it ★ in the panel. To change an email, edit that template in Omnisend, or save a copy as v2. "Prepare for Sy's approval" uses it automatically.

How to read the numbers

ACTUAL
Straight from Shopify orders or Meta spend you logged.
MODELED
Calculated with the plan's assumptions: $70 CAC, $0.85 per pair, $7.50 shipping. "Expected" on a move = people × their usual reorder rate × 1.5 campaign lift × average repeat order.
ESTIMATE
A media budget scenario, used when real Meta spend isn't logged for that period.
Colors
green on track · amber at risk or waiting · red off track or overdue.

Words used in the app

CDP
Customer data platform: one place that joins every customer's data and acts on it.
Profile
One customer, identified by their email address, with all their orders.
Audience
A group of customers picked by a rule, e.g. "1 order, last order 28–45 days ago". Each customer is in exactly one.
Segment
Omnisend's copy of an audience, limited to people subscribed to email.
Move
One action on Home, ranked by deadline and expected dollars.
Subscribed / consent
Whether someone agreed to marketing email. Only subscribed people get marketing. Sync it from Omnisend.
CAC
Cost to acquire a customer: Meta spend ÷ new customers. Target $70; above $85 every first order loses money.
AOV
Average order value. First-order target $90, which means leading with 18/24-pair bundles.
Repeat order
Any order after a customer's first. This is where the profit is (about $53 each).
LTV net / LTV profit
Everything a customer has spent after discounts and refunds / what's left after socks, shipping and the $70 CAC.
Wave A
The day 28–45 offer to first-time buyers: Club Starter, 6 pairs for $49, no discount code stacked.
C90
Win-back for one-order customers quiet 120+ days, with COMEBACK15 once.
Email cap
No more than 3 marketing emails per person per 30 days, and never Omnisend and Gmail marketing in the same week.
Sock size 13–15
Large-feet customers. It is a sock size, not an age.
